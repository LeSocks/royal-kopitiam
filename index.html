OUR HERITAGE • 东方皇家

A Modern Sanctuary for Classic Kopitiam Culture & Royal Oriental Flavors

At Royal Kopitiam (Kedai Kopitiam Royal Oriental), we bring together the enduring charm of classic kopitiam coffeehouses and authentic Oriental comfort dishes — where mornings begin with the clink of porcelain cups and evenings unfold around steaming plates shared with family and friends.

Designed with breezy louvered shutters, warm natural daylight, classic marble-topped bistro tables, and deep heritage green accents, our space is built to slow down time. Whether you are dropping by for a quick #51 Kaya Toast and #36 Kopi O or gathering over #02 Hainan Chicken Rice, #14 Char Kway Teow Prawn, and bamboo dim sum baskets, every dish is prepared fresh to order.

Is Royal Kopitiam 100% Halal-Friendly (No Pork, No Lard)? +

Yes. Every single item on the Royal Kopitiam (Kedai Kopitiam Royal Oriental) menu (#01 through #61) is prepared strictly 100% No Pork and No Lard. Our dim sum dumplings (#24 Siomay, #25 Hakau Prawn, #26 Lumpia Kulit Tahu), slow-simmered Ban Mee broths, and high-flame wok sauces are crafted exclusively with chicken, beef, prawn, and fresh vegetables.

What are the must-order signature dishes for first-time guests? +

For a classic morning visit, start with #51 Kaya Toast (26 K), #56 Soft Boiled Egg 2 Pcs (15 K), and #36 Kopi O (25 K) or #37 Kopi C (30 K). For lunch or dinner, our top-ordered mains are #01 Nasi Lemak with Fried Chicken Cutlet (40 K), #02 Hainan Chicken Rice White Chicken (40 K), #06 Nasi Ayam Goreng Kampung (55 K), smoky #14 Char Kway Teow Prawn (45 K), and #20 Pratha with Chicken Curry (42 K).

How much does a typical meal cost at Royal Kopitiam? +

Our menu ranges from 10 K to 55 K (Rp 10.000 – Rp 55.000). A complete morning breakfast set of Kaya Toast, Soft Boiled Eggs, and Kopi O is just 66 K (Rp 66.000), while a hearty lunch or dinner main dish with a specialty drink typically costs between 60 K and 80 K (Rp 60.000 – Rp 80.000) per person.

What is the difference between Kopi O, Kopi C, Teh Tarik, and Yuan Yang? +

#36 Kopi O (25 K) is dark sock-brewed coffee sweetened with cane sugar (no dairy). #37 Kopi C (30 K) adds silky evaporated milk for a smooth, balanced cup. #38 Teh Tarik (22 K) is Ceylon black tea pulled high with condensed and evaporated milk for a frothy top. #39 Yuan Yang (30 K) combines rich sock-brewed coffee with pulled milk tea in one cup. We also serve modern iced signatures like #44 Aren Latte (35 K) and #45 Pandan Latte (25 K).

Does Royal Kopitiam have Wi-Fi, power outlets, a Musholla, and group seating? +

Yes. We offer both an air-conditioned Royal Oriental dining hall and a breezy, sunlit veranda with round marble family tables and 2–4 person bistro tables. Guests enjoy complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, accessible power outlets, a clean Musholla (prayer room), and convenient courtyard parking.
