OUR STORY • 东方皇家

Classic Kopitiam Tradition & Oriental Comfort Food

Royal Kopitiam (Kedai Kopitiam Royal Oriental) serves traditional coffeehouse favorites alongside everyday Oriental comfort dishes, from early-morning coffee and toast to shared family meals.

Our dining room features louvered timber shutters, natural daylight, and marble-topped bistro tables. Stop by for #51 Kaya Toast and #36 Kopi O in the morning, or gather for #02 Hainan Chicken Rice, #14 Char Kway Teow Prawn, and steamed dim sum baskets.

No Pork • No Lard

Every stock, dim sum filling, wok sauce, and marinade is prepared without pork or lard.

Wok-Fried to Order

Char Kway Teow, Nasi Goreng, and Mee Goreng are cooked to order over a high flame.

Sock-Brewed Kopi & Teh

Dark-roast kopitiam coffee and Ceylon tea brewed through cloth filters and pulled to order.

Indoor & Veranda Seating

Air-conditioned main hall and semi-outdoor tables with Wi-Fi and power outlets.

Is Royal Kopitiam 100% Halal-Friendly (No Pork, No Lard)? +

Yes. Every item on the Royal Kopitiam (Kedai Kopitiam Royal Oriental) menu (#01 through #61) is prepared with no pork and no lard. Our dim sum dumplings (#24 Siomay, #25 Hakau Prawn, #26 Lumpia Kulit Tahu), Ban Mee broths, and wok sauces are made with chicken, beef, prawn, and vegetables.

What do you recommend for first-time guests? +

For breakfast, we recommend #51 Kaya Toast (26 K), #56 Soft Boiled Egg 2 Pcs (15 K), and #36 Kopi O (25 K) or #37 Kopi C (30 K). For lunch or dinner, popular mains include #01 Nasi Lemak with Fried Chicken Cutlet (40 K), #02 Hainan Chicken Rice White Chicken (40 K), #06 Nasi Ayam Goreng Kampung (55 K), #14 Char Kway Teow Prawn (45 K), and #20 Pratha with Chicken Curry (42 K).

How much does a typical meal cost? +

Menu items range from 10 K to 55 K (Rp 10.000 – Rp 55.000). A breakfast of Kaya Toast, Soft Boiled Eggs, and Kopi O is 66 K (Rp 66.000), while a main dish with a drink typically comes to 60 K – 80 K (Rp 60.000 – Rp 80.000) per person.

What is the difference between Kopi O, Kopi C, Teh Tarik, and Yuan Yang? +

#36 Kopi O (25 K) is black sock-brewed coffee with sugar and no milk. #37 Kopi C (30 K) is coffee with unsweetened evaporated milk and sugar. #38 Teh Tarik (22 K) is Ceylon black tea pulled with condensed and evaporated milk. #39 Yuan Yang (30 K) blends kopitiam coffee and pulled milk tea in one cup. We also serve iced lattes including #44 Aren Latte (35 K) and #45 Pandan Latte (25 K).

Do you have Wi-Fi, power outlets, a Musholla, and group seating? +

Yes. We have an air-conditioned main dining hall, a covered veranda, and a private VIP room. Guests have access to Wi-Fi, power outlets, a Musholla (prayer room), and courtyard parking.
